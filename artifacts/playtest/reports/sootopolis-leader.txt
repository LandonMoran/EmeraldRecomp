10:32Z) wrote its header and the post-fight reads but no speech: the fight ran
across two pad sessions and the one that won recorded nothing. The lines below
are a **re-fight of the same gym**, captured with the transcript appending every
utterance it heard.

  * checkpoint `sootopolis-cleared` loaded - the save is already past this gym;
  * the party was **healed at Sootopolis's POKeMON CENTER** first, because the
    first re-capture wedged on a move list where every move had 0 PP
    (`PECK, FLYING, PP 0 of 35. 3 of 4` on every press);
  * the three flags that gate the fight were cleared so he would battle again:
    `FLAG_BADGE08_GET` (2158), `FLAG_DEFEATED_SOOTOPOLIS_GYM` (1271) and
    `TRAINER_JUAN_1`'s own trainer flag (1552);
  * the developer warp to (8,3) facing him - the tester's hands, the same
    allowance every other gym row uses.
